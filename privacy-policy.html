Block Forge (the “Game”) is a single-player block puzzle game published by Wesan Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what data the Game processes and why. By installing or using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1 The short version

  • We do not require an account and never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To do this, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier and ad-interaction data.
  • We collect anonymous diagnostic and usage data (e.g. crashes, performance, session length, score events) to improve the Game. It is not tied to your identity.
  • There are no real-money purchases. In-game coins are earned through play and have no cash value.
  • The Game is not directed at children under 13.

2 Data we process

What the Game and its providers process, and for what purpose.
Data Purpose Who handles it

Our diagnostic data does not include your name, email, precise location, contacts, or photos, and it is not linked to a personal profile.

3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects for advertising. Learn more in Google’s advertising policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can control ad personalization at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → “Opt out of Ads Personalization”, or reset/delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, to allow or deny tracking. If Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt appears and you decline it, the ads you see are non-personalized.
  • Where required by law (e.g. the EEA/UK), you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

4 Legal bases (EEA / UK)

If you are in the EEA or the UK, we rely on these legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Consent or legitimate interest — for advertising, depending on your region and choices.
  • Legitimate interest — for anonymous diagnostics that keep the Game stable and improving.

5 Retention & international transfers

Anonymous diagnostic events are kept only as long as needed to analyse and improve the Game, then aggregated or deleted. Data held by Google AdMob is governed by Google’s own retention policies. Your on-device data is removed when you uninstall the Game.

Our service providers (Google and Supabase) may process data on servers located outside your country, including outside the EEA/UK, under the safeguards those providers apply.

6 Your rights & data deletion

Because we do not collect personal identifiers, most data cannot be tied back to you individually. You can still contact us to ask questions or request deletion of any diagnostic data associated with your device, and you can clear all local data simply by uninstalling the Game. Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal data.

Requests: canakyuz23@gmail.com

7 Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date at the top of the page.

3 Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Oyun, reklam göstermek için Google AdMob kullanır. Google, reklamcılık için topladığı veriler üzerinde bağımsız veri sorumlusudur. Ayrıntı: Google reklam politikası ve Google Gizlilik Politikası.

Reklam kişiselleştirmesini istediğiniz zaman kontrol edebilirsiniz:

  • Android: Ayarlar → Google → Reklamlar → “Reklam Kişiselleştirmeyi kapat” veya reklam kimliğini sıfırlama.
  • iOS: Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve Güvenlik → İzleme bölümünden izlemeye izin verebilir veya reddedebilirsiniz. Apple’ın İzleme Şeffaflığı (ATT) istemi görünür ve reddederseniz gördüğünüz reklamlar kişiselleştirilmez.
  • Yasaların gerektirdiği yerlerde (örn. AEA/Birleşik Krallık) kişiselleştirilmiş reklam öncesi onayınız istenebilir.

4 Yasal dayanaklar ve KVKK

AEA veya Birleşik Krallık’taysanız, GDPR kapsamında şu yasal dayanaklara dayanırız:
